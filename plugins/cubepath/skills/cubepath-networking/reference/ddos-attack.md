# cubecli ddos-attack

Manage DDoS attack information

## `cubecli ddos-attack details`

Show the details of an attack

Usage: `cubecli ddos-attack details <attack_id>`

## `cubecli ddos-attack list`

List DDoS attacks

Usage: `cubecli ddos-attack list`

## `cubecli ddos-attack traffic`

Show the traffic time series of an attack

Usage: `cubecli ddos-attack traffic <attack_id>`
